# SkillProof AI

### AI-Assisted Evidence-Linked Practical Skill Assessment

SkillProof AI is an AI-assisted platform designed to make practical vocational skill assessment more structured, consistent and evidence-based.

## Problem

Practical vocational skills are often evaluated through manual observation, making assessment time-consuming and potentially inconsistent. Final scores may also provide limited evidence of which specific steps or competencies were demonstrated.

## Proposed Solution

SkillProof AI allows a learner to record a predefined practical task using a smartphone. Computer vision analyzes the video to identify observable movements, tool usage, actions and safety cues.

The observations are mapped to a task-specific competency rubric and converted into timestamped evidence with confidence levels. A trainer reviews the flagged moments and confirms or overrides the AI's observations.

The verified assessment can then be stored as a structured digital skill record.

## How It Works

Learner records task
→ Video analysis
→ Observable evidence extraction
→ Rubric matching
→ Evidence + confidence + timestamps
→ Trainer verification
→ Digital skill record

## MVP Scope

The MVP focuses on one predefined practical task.

The initial prototype will demonstrate:

- Smartphone-based video capture
- Computer-vision-based observation
- Task-specific competency rubric
- Timestamped evidence
- Confidence scores
- Trainer confirmation/override
- Structured skill record
